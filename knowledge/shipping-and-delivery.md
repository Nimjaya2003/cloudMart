# Shipping and Delivery

## What delivery options do you offer?
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days and costs $4.99. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $12.99. International delivery takes 7 to 14 business days and starts at $19.99.

## Is there free shipping?
Standard delivery is free on orders of $75 or more. The $75 threshold is calculated per order, after discounts and before tax.

## When is my order dispatched?
Orders placed before 14:00 UTC on a business day are dispatched the same day. Orders placed later are dispatched the next business day.

## How do I track my order?
We email a tracking number within 24 hours of dispatch. The same tracking link is shown on the Orders page of your account.

## Do you ship internationally, and who pays customs duties?
We ship to over 40 countries. Import duties and taxes are not included in the shipping price and are paid by the recipient on delivery.

## Can you deliver to a PO box?
PO boxes are supported for Standard delivery only. Express and international parcels need a street address.

## What if my parcel has not arrived?
If your parcel has not arrived 10 business days after the estimated delivery date, contact customer support and we will open a carrier investigation.
