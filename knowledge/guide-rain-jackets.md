# Rain Jacket Guide

This guide covers the Lightweight Rain Jacket, a packable waterproof jacket with taped seams and an adjustable hood.

## What does a waterproof rating mean?
Waterproof ratings are given in millimetres (mm) and show how much water pressure the fabric resists. A rating of 10,000 mm handles steady rain on a day hike. Ratings of 20,000 mm or more suit heavy rain and long exposure. Taped seams matter as much as the rating, because water enters through stitching holes.

## How should I wear it?
Adjust the hood so you can turn your head without the hood turning with it. Wear a thin fleece or wool layer under the jacket when it is cold. Avoid thick cotton layers underneath, because they stay wet.

## How do I wash a waterproof jacket?
Close all zips and wash the jacket at 30 degrees Celsius with a technical wash product. Do not use fabric softener or bleach, because they damage the waterproof coating. Rinse twice, then tumble dry on a low heat or hang it to dry.

## The jacket soaks up water on the outside. Is it broken?
Not necessarily. The outer fabric has a water repellent finish that wears off with use and dirt, so water stops beading. Wash the jacket, then heat it briefly in a tumble dryer on low to reactivate the finish. Re-proof the jacket with a spray-on treatment every year or two.

## How do I pack and store it?
The Lightweight Rain Jacket packs small into your bag. Store it dry and loosely folded or hung, not compressed for months.
