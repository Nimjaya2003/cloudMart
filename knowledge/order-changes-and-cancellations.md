# Order Changes and Cancellations

## Can I cancel my order after placing it?
You can cancel an order from the Orders page of your account until it is dispatched. Orders are dispatched quickly (see the Shipping and Delivery document), so cancel as soon as possible. A cancelled order is refunded in full.

## What if my order has already been dispatched?
A dispatched order cannot be cancelled. You can refuse the delivery or return the items after they arrive, and the normal return policy applies.

## Can I change the delivery address?
The delivery address can only be changed before dispatch. Contact customer support immediately with your order number and the new address. After dispatch, the address cannot be changed.

## Can I add an item to an existing order?
Items cannot be added to an existing order. Place a new order instead. Free shipping thresholds are calculated separately for each order.

## Will I get a confirmation of my order?
Yes. An order confirmation email is sent as soon as you place the order. If you do not receive it, check your spam folder and the email address you entered at checkout.
