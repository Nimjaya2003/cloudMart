# Store Information and Contact

## Who is CloudMart?
CloudMart is an online-only shop for outdoor gear. We do not have physical stores.

## What are the customer support hours?
Customer support is open Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 18:00 UTC and Saturday from 10:00 to 14:00 UTC. We are closed on Sundays.

## How do I contact customer support?
Email support@cloudmart.example or use live chat on the website during support hours. We do not offer phone support. Include your order number so we can help faster.

## How quickly will you reply?
We reply to emails within 1 business day. Warranty claims with photos receive a reply within 2 business days.

## How do I speak to a manager or escalate a complaint?
Reply to your support email and write "Escalation" at the start of the subject line. A senior team member reviews the case and replies within 3 business days.
