# Socks and Layering Guide

This guide covers the Merino Wool Socks, breathable hiking socks that stay warm when wet.

## Why merino socks instead of cotton?
Merino wool moves moisture away from your skin, stays warm when damp, and resists odour. Cotton absorbs sweat, stays wet and causes blisters, so avoid cotton socks for hiking.

## How should hiking socks fit?
Socks should be snug without bunching or wrinkles, because wrinkles cause friction. Choose a mid-weight sock for hiking boots and a lighter sock for trail shoes in warm weather.

## How do I wash merino socks?
Turn the socks inside out and wash at 30 to 40 degrees Celsius. Do not use bleach or fabric softener. Dry them flat or tumble dry on low heat. Rotate between two or three pairs on multi-day trips.

## What is a basic layering system for hiking?
Use three layers. A base layer next to the skin moves sweat away, a mid layer such as fleece keeps you warm, and a waterproof shell such as a rain jacket protects against wind and rain. Add or remove layers before you get too hot or too cold.
