# Account and Order FAQ

## Where can I see my past orders?
Open the Orders page in your account. It lists every order with its status, items and total.

## What do the order statuses mean?
Placed means we received your order. Packed means it is being prepared at the warehouse. Shipped means it has been handed to the carrier. Delivered means the carrier confirmed delivery. Returned means we received a return and are processing it.

## I forgot my password. What do I do?
Choose Forgot password on the sign-in page and enter your email address. We send a reset link that is valid for 30 minutes. If you do not see the email, check your spam folder.

## Do I need an account to place an order?
No. Guest checkout is available. Creating an account lets you view past orders, track parcels and start returns online.

## How do I change my email address?
Open Account settings and select Edit email. We send a confirmation link to the new address, and the change takes effect once you click it.

## How do I delete my account?
Contact customer support from the email address on the account and ask for deletion. We delete personal data within 30 days, except records we must keep for tax purposes.
