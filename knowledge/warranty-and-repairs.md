# Warranty and Repairs

## What does the warranty cover?
All CloudMart products carry a 12 month warranty against manufacturing defects, counted from the delivery date. Covered defects include faulty stitching, broken zips, failed seams, sole separation on footwear, and electrical faults in rechargeable products.

## What is not covered?
The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, damage from misuse or accidents, damage caused by washing against the care instructions, cuts and punctures, or the gradual loss of battery capacity that is normal for rechargeable products.

## How do I make a warranty claim?
Contact customer support with your order number, a short description of the problem, and clear photos of the defect. Contact details and reply times are in the Store Information document.

## What happens after I submit a claim?
If the claim is approved, we choose between a repair, a replacement or a refund. When we need the item back, we pay for shipping. If a claim is declined, we explain why, and you can still use the normal return policy if you are within the return window.

## Does the warranty cover boots that wear out after heavy use?
No. Soles and linings wear down with use, and this is not a defect. Sole separation, where the sole peels away from the boot, is a defect and is covered for 12 months.
