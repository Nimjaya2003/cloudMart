# Returns and Refunds

## How long do I have to return an item?
You can return unused items within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund.

## Can I return footwear I have already worn?
Footwear that has only been tried on indoors can be returned within 30 days. Footwear with visible outdoor wear, such as mud or sole scuffing, cannot be returned unless it is defective. Defective items are handled under the warranty policy.

## Which items cannot be returned?
Socks can only be returned if the pack is unopened and unworn. Gift cards cannot be returned. Items damaged by the customer after delivery cannot be returned.

## Who pays for return shipping?
CloudMart pays for return shipping when the item is defective or we sent the wrong item. For all other returns, a flat return label fee of $5.00 is deducted from the refund.

## How long does a refund take?
We refund the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item arrives at our warehouse. Your bank may need extra time to show the refund.

## Can I exchange an item for a different size?
Yes. Size exchanges are free within 30 days of delivery. Start the exchange from the Orders page of your account, and we ship the replacement once the carrier scans your parcel.

## How do I start a return?
Open the Orders page in your account, choose the order, and select Return item. You can also contact customer support.
