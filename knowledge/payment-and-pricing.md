# Payment and Pricing

## Which payment methods do you accept?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal. We do not offer cash on delivery or bank transfers.

## When is my card charged?
Your card is authorised at checkout and charged when your order is dispatched. If an order is cancelled before dispatch, the authorisation is released within 3 to 5 business days.

## Are taxes included in the listed prices?
Listed prices are in US dollars and do not include sales tax or VAT. Tax is calculated from your delivery address and shown at checkout before you pay.

## What if a price drops after I buy?
If the price of an item you bought drops within 14 days of purchase, contact customer support for a price adjustment. We refund the difference to your original payment method. Clearance items are not eligible.

## Can I use more than one promo code?
Only one promo code can be used per order. Promo codes cannot be applied to an order after it has been placed.

## Why was my payment declined?
Common causes are incorrect card details, insufficient funds, or a bank security check. Try again or use another payment method. A declined payment does not create an order and you are not charged.
