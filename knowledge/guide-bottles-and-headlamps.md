# Bottle and Headlamp Guide

This guide covers the Insulated Water Bottle (750 ml stainless steel) and the LED Headlamp (300 lumens, rechargeable, with a red night mode).

## How long does the insulated bottle keep drinks cold or hot?
The Insulated Water Bottle keeps drinks cold for up to 24 hours and hot for up to 12 hours when kept closed.

## How do I clean the bottle?
Wash the bottle with warm soapy water and a bottle brush after each trip. For stains or smells, soak it overnight with a spoonful of baking soda and warm water, then rinse. Hand wash the bottle and lid, and leave the lid off while they dry.

## How much water should I carry?
Plan on about 500 ml per hour of moderate hiking. For a three hour hike, carry about 1.5 litres, which is two 750 ml bottles.

## How long does the headlamp battery last?
On high (300 lumens) the LED Headlamp battery lasts about 3 hours. On low it lasts about 20 hours. In red night mode it lasts about 40 hours.

## How do I charge the headlamp?
Charge the LED Headlamp with a USB-C cable until the indicator turns green, which takes about 3 hours. Charge it fully before every trip and carry a small power bank on multi-day hikes.

## When should I use the red night mode?
Use red light at camp or on trails when you want to keep your night vision and avoid dazzling other hikers. Use white light for route finding and technical ground.
