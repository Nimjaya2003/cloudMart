# Buying Guide: Your First Day Hike

## What do I need for a dry day hike on an easy trail?
Wear comfortable hiking boots such as the Trail Hiking Boots with Merino Wool Socks. Carry an Insulated Water Bottle and enough food for the day.

## What should I add for a rainy day hike?
Add the Lightweight Rain Jacket, which packs small and goes over your other layers. Keep the Merino Wool Socks, because wet cotton socks cause blisters. Waterproof boots keep your feet dry in shallow puddles and wet grass.

## Do I need a headlamp for a day hike?
Carry a headlamp such as the LED Headlamp whenever there is any chance of finishing after sunset. Daylight ends sooner than most beginners expect, especially in forests or in winter.

## What else should I carry?
Bring a map or offline route, a small first aid kit with blister plasters, a warm layer, and a charged phone. Tell someone your route and expected return time.

## How do I choose between products?
Start with footwear and socks, because blisters end more hikes than anything else. Add a rain jacket and a water bottle next, and a headlamp for hikes that may run late.
